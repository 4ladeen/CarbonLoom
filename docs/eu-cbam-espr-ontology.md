# EU CBAM & ESPR Regulatory Ontology

**Domain:** Cross-Border Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) & Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR)

## 1. Harmonized System (HS) & Combined Nomenclature (CN) Mappings
- **Textiles & Apparel**: HS 6109 (T-shirts, singlets), HS 6203 (Men's suits, jackets, trousers), HS 6204 (Women's suits, dresses).
- **Industrial Precursors**: Iron & Steel (HS 7201-7229), Aluminium (HS 7601-7616), Fertilisers (HS 3102, 3105).

## 2. EU CBAM Definitive Rules (2026-2027)
- **Threshold**: 50-tonne de minimis mass threshold confirmed September 30, 2026.
- **Reference Price**: Q1/Q2 2026 benchmark EUR 75.36 / EUR 75.28 per tCO2e.
- **Default vs Actual**: Default marked-up emission factors penalize non-reporting suppliers by up to 28% compared to primary verified records.

## 3. ESPR Digital Product Passport (DPP) Architecture
- **Standards**: EN 18216 & EN 18219–18223 harmonised DPP standards.
- **Registry Integration**: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1778.
- **Mandatory Claims**: Bans generic claims ("eco-friendly", "carbon neutral"). All emissions claims require verifiable calculation lineage and cryptographic anchor hashes.
