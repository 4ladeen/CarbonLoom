## 1. Executive Summary, Vision & Market Imperative

### 1.1 Vision & Positioning
* **Mission:** Make every garment's—and industrial export's—energy and carbon footprint measurable, reducible, and cryptographically verifiable without leaking factory trade secrets.
* **Core Operational Loop:** $\text{Measure} \rightarrow \text{Explain} \rightarrow \text{Reduce} \rightarrow \text{Prove} \rightarrow \text{Govern}$.
* **One-Line Positioning:** CarbonLoom is an agentic energy, carbon accounting, and regulatory compliance operating system for export factories—eliminating invisible floor energy waste, computing per-garment emissions, and issuing tamper-evident, zero-knowledge proofs to global buyers.
* **Why "CarbonLoom":** A loom weaves separate threads into durable fabric. CarbonLoom weaves meters, bilingual utility bills, ERP runs, supplier inputs, and EU regulatory rules into one verifiable carbon fabric.

### 1.2 Verified 2026 Market & Regulatory Imperative
* **Bangladesh's EU Apparel Margin Pressure:** Bangladesh's RMG exports to the EU reached EUR 19.41 billion in 2025 ($+5.97\%$ YoY) and represented $48.98\%$ (USD 15.54 billion) of the USD 31.72 billion exported in July–April FY2025–26 [5]. However, between January and September 2026, EU-bound RMG exports contracted $11.91\%$ YoY to EUR 13.48 billion (an approx. EUR 1.82 billion decline) [5]. Simultaneously cutting factory utility waste and automating European buyer compliance is now critical to protecting net factory margins.
* **EU ESPR & 2026 Apparel Compliance Milestones:** The EU Digital Product Passport (DPP) Registry went live on July 20, 2026 under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1778, supported by harmonised DPP system standards EN 18216 and EN 18219–18223 (Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1736) [4]. Additional 2026 apparel rules include the July 19, 2026 ban on destroying unsold consumer apparel for large enterprises (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/296), the March 2, 2027 annual discard disclosure format (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2), bans on unsubstantiated generic green claims ("eco-friendly", "green", "carbon neutral"), and the October 10, 2026 EU PFHxA chemical restriction in consumer clothing [4]. Because the Textiles Delegated Act is expected in 2027 (with legally mandatory textile DPPs taking effect around mid-2029), CarbonLoom outputs **ESPR / EN 18216–18223 DPP-ready** passports today.
* **EU CBAM Definitive Regime (Composite & Heavy Industry):** Effective January 1, 2026 across iron, steel, cement, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity, and hydrogen, with the European Commission confirming the $50\text{-tonne}$ annual de minimis mass threshold on September 30, 2026 [3]. Q1 and Q2 2026 reference certificate prices reached EUR 75.36 and EUR 75.28 per tonne of $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$, with certificate sales opening February 1, 2027 and the first annual declaration due September 30, 2027 [3]. Factories lacking audit-traceable primary data face punitive marked-up EU default emission values.
* **Verified Bangladesh Local Emission Baselines:** Bangladesh's Department of Environment (DoE) official Grid Emission Factor (GEF) stands at $0.61\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$ (2019–20) and $0.62\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$ (2020–21), alongside Joint Crediting Mechanism (JCM) conservative captive generation references of $0.376\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$ (natural gas) and $0.533\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$ (diesel) [6].

1. **Carbon per Garment & Batch:** Allocates Scope 1/2/3 emissions to production line, style, PO, and individual piece using Standard Minute Value (SMV) weighting aligned with GHG Protocol [1] and ISO 14064-1 [2].

3. **Zero-LLM-Arithmetic & Deterministic Explainability (TreeSHAP):** LLMs are architecturally banned from performing carbon or financial math. Deterministic Python executes ISO 14064-1 equations; Isolation Forest + XGBoost + TreeSHAP detect energy waste and invoice-vs-telemetry fraud with exact feature attributions.
4. **Hybrid GraphRAG + Contextual & Corrective RAG:** Neo4j/Apache AGE legal & equipment topology combined with `BGE-M3` dense/BM25 search [8], `bge-reranker-v2-m3`, Contextual Retrieval [11], Corrective RAG grading [12], page-level citations, and a strict refusal rule.

7. **Offline-First Edge Mode:** Runs on an on-premise factory mini-PC (Mosquitto MQTT + local SQLite/NATS buffer + Ollama Qwen/Llama) paired with live ESP32 + SCT-013 current clamps for uninterrupted operation during ISP outages.
8. **Physics-Backed Optimization & CFO ROI Engine:** Integrates OR-Tools/Pyomo tariff-aware load shifting, `pvlib` [8] + NASA POWER solar/storage sizing, Marginal Abatement Cost Curves (MACC), and live EU CBAM tariff penalty simulations.

10. **Open Core, Worker Privacy & Compute Honesty:** Built on open standards; strictly line/machine-level sensing (zero worker surveillance or video analytics); explicit uncertainty ranges, data-quality tags, and transparent reporting of CarbonLoom's own AI compute energy footprint.

### 3.2 Canonical Request & Governance Flow
1. **Ingress:** A user submits a Bangla/English query, voice note, or document—or an MQTT meter anomaly triggers an automated event.
2. **Zero-Trust Policy Check:** `carbonloom-mcp-gateway` validates tenant JWT claims, strips PII/bank/TIN fields via Presidio, applies prompt-injection sanitization to uploaded file text, and enforces tenant rate/spend budgets.
3. **Model Tiering & Planning:** The Supervisor Agent routes simple classification, routing, and grading to a small local model (Ollama Qwen/Llama) and complex multi-step reasoning to a frontier model (Claude).
4. **Deterministic Execution & Grounded Retrieval:** Agents invoke read-only MCP servers (`meter-mcp`, `erp-mcp`, `factors-mcp`, `doc-vision-mcp`, `regulatory-rag-mcp`). All emission equations, uncertainty intervals, optimizations, and CBAM tax math execute strictly in deterministic Python. Hybrid GraphRAG + CRAG retrieves versioned regulatory and manual passages with page/node citations.
5. **Critic Gate:** The Critic Agent verifies that every numeric claim resolves to a deterministic calculation lineage ID and every regulatory claim cites a verified source—otherwise enforcing refusal.
6. **Human Approval & Cryptographic Commit:** Any mutating action (creating a CMMS maintenance ticket, dispatching a Tier-2 WhatsApp link, issuing a buyer report, generating a ZK passport, or anchoring on Hyperledger Fabric) pauses at a LangGraph interrupt node and emits an interactive **Approval Card**. Upon human confirmation, a single-use approval token unlocks the write MCP tool, logs a SHA-256 hash-chained audit event, records OpenTelemetry/Langfuse spans, and anchors the batch Merkle root on Fabric.

### Module A: Omnichannel Ingestion & Connectors

Outputs Pydantic JSON with bounding boxes and per-field confidence scores; any field with confidence $<0.90$ routes to the human review queue.
* **FR-A3 ERP & Production Import `[Designed]`:** Ingest POs, styles, Standard Minute Values (SMV), fiber/alloy BOMs, pieces cut/sewn/packed, and shipment tonnage via CSV, XLSX, and read-only SQL/REST API with Postgres RLS.

* **FR-A5 External Data Feeds `[Designed]`:** Scheduled ingestion of utility tariff schedules, local weather, BPDB/DoE/JCM/IPCC emission factors, NASA POWER solar irradiance, and live EU ETS carbon auction reference prices.

### Module B: Deterministic Carbon Accounting Engine
* **FR-B1 Versioned Boundary & Factor Library `[Designed]`:** Version-controlled emission factors tagged by authority, geography, and year (e.g., Bangladesh DoE GEF $0.62\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$, JCM captive natural gas $0.376$ / diesel $0.533\text{ tCO}_2/\text{MWh}$, IPCC AR6).
* **FR-B2 Scope 1 Engine `[Designed]`:** Deterministic Python accounting for steam boilers, captive gas generators, diesel gensets, and refrigerant top-ups [1].
* **FR-B3 Scope 2 Engine `[Designed]`:** Dual reporting supporting location-based and market-based accounting (including rooftop solar self-consumption and renewable PPAs) [1].
* **FR-B4 Scope 3 Starter & Cascade `[Designed]`:** Blends Tier-2/3 supplier cascade actuals with documented fallback estimates for fabric, trims, yarn, steel precursors, and freight—each tagged with data quality.
* **FR-B5 SMV Carbon-per-Garment Allocation Engine `[Designed]`:** Allocates energy and emissions down to production line, style, order, and individual piece. Default allocation weight for order $j$ is:
  $$w_j = \frac{\text{Pieces}_j \times \text{SMV}_j}{\sum_{k=1}^{M} (\text{Pieces}_k \times \text{SMV}_k)}$$
  Alternative physical-mass and machine-runtime allocation rules are user-selectable.
* **FR-B6 Uncertainty Quantification & Data-Quality Tagging `[Designed]`:** Every emitted metric outputs a numeric uncertainty range $[\text{low}, \text{mid}, \text{high}]$ plus an immutable provenance tag (`measured`, `billed`, `estimated`, or `simulated`).
* **FR-B7 Click-Through Calculation Lineage `[Designed]`:** Selecting any metric opens its complete dependency tree: formula version, factor ID/year, raw bill/meter slice, and SHA-256 hash.
* **FR-B8 Standards Alignment `[Designed]`:** Report templates follow GHG Protocol Corporate Standard [1] and ISO 14064-1:2018 [2] structures while transparently disclaiming certification status.
