Frequently Asked Questions

Common Governance Questions

How does CarbonLoom protect supplier unit pricing?

CarbonLoom compiles emission equations into a Circom 2 arithmetic circuit. The EU buyer or customs authority receives a zero-knowledge Groth16 proof demonstrating that emissions satisfy the benchmark without disclosing raw unit costs or spinning mill names.

Does CarbonLoom require replacing existing factory meters?

No. CarbonLoom interfaces via non-invasive split-core current transformer (CT) clamps (ESP32 SCT-013) and reads Modbus RS-485 outputs from existing steam, gas, and power meters without halting production.

What is the Zero-LLM-Arithmetic Law?

Non-negotiable architectural invariant: No neural model or LLM is ever permitted to calculate greenhouse gas emissions, SMV fractions, or penalty tariffs. All math executes in deterministic Python with immutable SHA-256 lineage hash chaining.

How are audits conducted across Frankfurt and Dhaka?

Non-Resident Bangladeshi (NRB) certified sustainability engineers review low-confidence document flags and high-SHAP anomalies in the Auditor Workspace, co-signing Ed25519 attestations on the Hyperledger Fabric ledger under NDA.
