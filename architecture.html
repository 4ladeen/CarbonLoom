Cryptographic Architecture & Engineering Governance

A zero-trust operating system engineered to execute deterministic greenhouse gas arithmetic, preserve supplier commercial secrecy via Circom zk-SNARKs, and anchor audit lineage to Hyperledger Fabric.

1. Zero-LLM-Arithmetic Law (CLAUDE.md §1)

Under no circumstances shall an LLM, neural agent, or heuristic model calculate emissions, SMV allocations, baseline normalizations, or EU CBAM tariff penalties. All equations execute in deterministic, audited Python modules located in services/api/core/ with an arithmetic tolerance of exactly 0.00%.

lineage_id = SHA-256(formula_v2 + meter_slice + factor_id) Deterministic

2. Circom 2 Groth16 zk-SNARKs

Enables export factories to prove that batch emissions satisfy EU CBAM benchmarks (≤ 2.50 kgCO2e/piece) while mathematically concealing Tier-2 spinning mills, dyeing chemistry, and FOB yarn margins.

Circuit: cbam_proof.circom (BN254) 142 ms WASM

3. Forensic TreeSHAP Waste Attribution

Isolation Forest and XGBoost feature attributions isolate physical energy leaks from ambient weather fluctuations or line speed. Detects failed steam trap orifices and compressor leaks with >0.70 confidence before escalating to human auditors.

BOILER-01 Trap Blow-by Attribution: 84.2% Score: 0.85

4. Go MCP Policy Gateway & Fabric Ledger

All agent tool calls pass through carbonloom-mcp-gateway in Go. Presidio PII scrubbing redacts bank accounts and phones, while single-use Ed25519 approval tokens prevent un-audited writes to the Hyperledger Fabric ledger.
